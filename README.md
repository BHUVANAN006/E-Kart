# E-Kart

### Electric Kart Design and Development

E-Kart is an electric vehicle development project focused on the design, modelling, integration, and prototype development of an electric kart. The project combines mechanical design, CAD modelling, structural analysis, component integration, and prototype validation to develop a functional electric kart system.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The E-Kart project involves the engineering design and development of an electric kart with emphasis on chassis development, mechanical component design, vehicle integration, and performance validation.

The project follows a systematic engineering workflow from concept development and CAD modelling to fabrication, assembly, testing, and prototype evaluation.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Develop a functional electric kart prototype.
- Design and develop the kart chassis and mechanical systems.
- Perform 3D CAD modelling and assembly development.
- Integrate steering, braking, suspension, drivetrain, and electrical systems.
- Evaluate structural performance through engineering analysis.
- Develop a manufacturable and practical vehicle design.
- Validate the developed prototype through testing.
- Apply mechanical engineering principles to electric vehicle development.
